RECORD YOUR OWN NARRATION
=========================

Save your recording in this folder as:  narration.mp3
(the app auto-detects it; until then it uses the browser voice)

Suggested script to read aloud:

  "Welcome. This question is hidden on purpose.
   Click the eye button to rotate the flipped text until it stands upright.
   Hold the Control key and press R to slide the shapes away and uncover the words.
   If you move your cursor off the sheet, everything hides again.
   Read the question carefully, then solve the Python challenge."

.wav or .m4a also work — just rename the file to narration.mp3, or tell me
the extension and I'll point the app at it.
